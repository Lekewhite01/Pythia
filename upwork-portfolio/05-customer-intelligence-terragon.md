## The Problem

A martech company operating across Africa needed ML-driven customer intelligence capabilities to power targeted marketing, audience building, and campaign optimization for enterprise clients. The platform processed millions of customer records and transaction data points, but lacked the ML layer to extract actionable segments, predict customer behavior, and optimize campaign ROI.

## What I Built

I designed and deployed production ML systems that powered the company's customer intelligence and personalization capabilities.

- **Customer Segmentation Engine** — Built clustering and classification models that segmented millions of customers based on behavioral patterns, transaction history, and demographic signals. Segments directly fed into campaign targeting and audience selection.

- **Lookalike Audience Models** — Developed ML models that identified potential high-value customers who resembled existing best customers, enabling enterprise clients to expand their reach with precision targeting.

- **Credit-Related Recommendations** — Built scoring models that assessed customer propensity for financial products, enabling cross-sell and up-sell recommendations within the platform.

- **End-to-End ML Pipelines** — Designed pipelines from data ingestion and feature engineering through model deployment via REST APIs, processing millions of records efficiently using Apache Spark for distributed computation.

- **Stack Modernization** — Helped modernize the team's ML infrastructure for scalability and long-term maintainability, including containerization, API-based model serving, and automated retraining.

## Results

- Segmentation models directly improved campaign performance and engagement rates for enterprise clients.
- Lookalike models expanded targetable audiences while maintaining conversion quality.
- ML pipelines processed millions of customer records reliably, enabling near-real-time scoring.
- Platform modernization reduced model deployment complexity and improved iteration speed for the data science team.
